LightAPI

LightAPI turns a directory of more than 1,500 free public APIs into an active monitoring platform. Visitors can discover an API, add it to an isolated workspace, and immediately track uptime and latency.

Readings stream to the dashboard over WebSockets, while per-endpoint anomaly detection learns normal behavior and flags unusual performance. The platform also includes incident history, predictive alerts, security hardening, and a Python monitoring SDK.

Frameworks & toolsReact · Vite · Tailwind CSS · Recharts · FastAPI · SQLAlchemy · SQLite · APScheduler · WebSockets · PyTorch · scikit-learn

FootyLive

FootyLive brings live scores, fixtures, standings, top scorers, team information, and match history together across 12 football competitions.

Its tournament experience includes themed knockout brackets, two-leg aggregation, penalty shootouts, and champion states. Finished seasons are archived locally so tables and brackets remain available without repeatedly calling the external API.

CrimeScope

CrimeScope transforms live incident records from 17 U.S. city data portals into an interactive geographic exploration tool.

Users can move between individual pins, clustered incidents, and heatmap views, filter by severity and time of day, and share URL-synced views. The fully client-side app normalizes data from both Socrata and ArcGIS sources and communicates when a portal is not current.

Frameworks & toolsReact · Vite · Leaflet · React Leaflet · Zustand · Recharts · date-fns · Socrata SODA API · ArcGIS FeatureServer

SharpEdge

SharpEdge is a sportsbook comparison dashboard that brings live lines from nine books into one interface across the NFL, NBA, MLB, and NHL.

Demand-driven polling keeps data collection focused on the markets people are actually viewing, while line-movement history makes it easier to see how prices shift between books over time.

PokePrice

PokePrice is a full-stack Pokémon TCG tracker that combines current market prices with historical charts for individual cards.

Search and filter state lives in the URL so useful views can be bookmarked or shared. A snapshot engine records TCGPlayer prices over time, turning changing listings into market history that can be explored visually.

Apostles4Jesus

I built a complete bilingual teaching platform for Apostles4Jesus, a real Orthodox Christian community with more than 15,000 followers across social media.

The site provides five fully mirrored pages in English and Arabic with true RTL layouts, an episode library, live-stream detection, team and mission pages, and a secured serverless contact form. It is also installable as a PWA, works offline, and includes performance, accessibility, security, and multilingual SEO work.
